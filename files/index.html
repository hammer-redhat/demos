Ansible Demo

Infrastructure as code · agentless automation

This page is a simple landing spot for an Ansible walkthrough: playbooks, inventory, and idempotent tasks against your lab hosts.

What you are automating

Ansible runs SSH (or WinRM) against hosts you define, applies modules in order, and reports changed vs. ok vs. failed tasks—no agent install required on targets.

  • Inventory lists hosts and groups (static files or dynamic plugins).
  • Playbooks are YAML: plays, tasks, roles, and handlers.
  • Modules do the real work (packages, files, services, templates, and more).

Typical demo flow

From your control node, syntax-check and dry-run before applying changes.

ansible-playbook site.yml --syntax-check
ansible-playbook site.yml --check --diff

Remove --check when you are ready to apply for real.
